The M.O.S method

Every room is a record.

No fixed playlist survives contact with a real floor. Each room gets read, then written: a street, a forest, a desert, a kiosk in the Parc Royal. Six chapters from the field.

01

La Rue

"on veut danser la nuit"

A square in Brussels turns into a floor in one afternoon. Open air, zero ceiling, the mix leans sunny and loud until the street sings back.

02

La Forêt

hidden in the forest

Voodoo Village, 2023 and 2024. A tribe hidden in the trees outside Brussels, two years running, on bills with Adriatique, WhoMadeWho and Nicole Moudaber.

03

Le Désert

no man's world, avec amour

A desert festival, sand for a dancefloor, sharing the bill with Marc Romboy and Jonathan Kaspar. Closing-set energy with nowhere to hide.

04

On Air

bonjour le parc royal

Kiosk Radio broadcasts from a wooden kiosk in Brussels' Parc Royal. Multiple sessions, latest 02/26, each one a different record for the same little room.

05

Le Club

jusqu'aux lumières

Hangar's MIND parties, Jeux d'Hiver, Fondation Boghossian. The late rooms where the closing set is the whole point: high-energy, emotional, built to keep a floor present until the lights come up.

06

Après

la suite au prochain disque

After hours, private floors, Munich to Los Angeles. The rooms nobody photographs properly because everybody is dancing.

The artist

M.O.S.

Michael Oulbaz Suarez, Bruxelles

M.O.S is a DJ and producer from Brussels. His sets move through eclectic, bass-forward house built on emotional edits, with selections shaped to the room in front of him rather than a fixed playlist.

The proof is in the range: two editions of Voodoo Village, a desert festival with Marc Romboy, Hangar's MIND parties, art-foundation evenings at Fondation Boghossian, broadcast sessions for Kiosk Radio and Jam Radio, and floors from Munich to Los Angeles. Same selector, different record every time.

His closing sets are the signature: high-energy, emotional, built to keep a floor present until the lights come up.
