Michael Oulbaz Suarez

M.O.S builds sets the way he collects records: slowly, by ear, keeping only what moves a room.

Born of African roots and based in Brussels, he folds R&B warmth and Eastern lines into deep, physical house. Vinyl and hardware sit next to the digital rig; tradition and modernity get mixed in the same channel.

On stage that translates into patient, hypnotic sets that read the floor rather than fight it, from club rooms to open-air stages and the Kiosk Radio booth in Brussels park.

Three productions are on the way for 2026, built from samples and machines, with a label and a party series behind them.
