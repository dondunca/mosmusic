About M.O.S

M.O.S builds sets the way he collects records: slowly, by ear, keeping only what moves a room.

Born of African roots and based in Brussels, M.O.S folds R&B warmth and Eastern lines into deep, physical house. Vinyl and hardware sit beside the digital rig; tradition and modernity are mixed in the same channel. His sets are patient and hypnotic, built to read the floor rather than fight it, from club rooms to open-air stages and the Kiosk Radio booth.

The name belongs to Michael Oulbaz Suarez, a selector who came to production through years of listening: analog records, East African and Eastern tonalities, the R&B he grew up on, and the deep end of house music. That mix gives his sets their signature, seductive rhythms under hypnotic patience, groove before spectacle.

On stage he moves between vinyl, hardware and CDJs. In 2026 he appeared on Kiosk Radio in Brussels park and self-released a run of tracks, Amour Seum, Cataclysme 2.0 and Automatic Sex, while playing club rooms, open-air stages and a run of private dates in Belgium and beyond.

Three new productions are in the works, sample-driven and machine-built. Behind them sits a longer plan: a label and a party series of his own, built the same way as the sets, piece by piece, with nothing in the bag that doesn't earn its place.
